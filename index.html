About Us

AlgaeAfrica Biotech is an early-stage biotechnology company developing algae-based solutions for sustainable agriculture and future bio-based industries in Africa.

The company is currently focused on developing algae-derived biostimulants that improve crop performance and soil health, while building toward a long-term algae biorefinery platform for high-value products such as natural pigments, food ingredients, and nutraceutical compounds.

Our Mission

To develop scalable algae-based technologies that support sustainable agriculture, resource-efficient production, and Africa’s emerging bioeconomy.

Our Vision

To become Africa’s leading algae biotechnology company, transforming algae biomass into sustainable agricultural and biorefinery products.

Why Choose Us?

Expertise in algae biotechnology and biorefinery systems, with a focus on scalable, locally adaptable, and sustainability-driven solutions for Africa.

Our Solutions

Algae-Based Biostimulants

Developing algae-derived biostimulants that support root development, crop performance, and soil health through naturally occurring bioactive compounds.

Algae Biorefinery

Building future biorefinery systems for the production of high-value algae compounds including phycobiliproteins, food ingredients, and bio-based products.

Biomass Supply & Cultivation

Developing scalable algae biomass production networks through partnerships with growers and coastal communities across Africa.

Research & Consulting

Providing technical support in algae cultivation, bioprocessing, and circular bioeconomy systems.

Our Growth Model

Phase 1 — Market Entry

Launching algae-based biostimulants for sustainable agriculture and early farmer adoption.

Phase 2 — Biorefinery Expansion

Expanding into higher-value algae products including pigments, nutraceutical compounds, and food ingredients.

Phase 3 — Biomass Networks

Building distributed algae and seaweed biomass supply systems through local production partnerships.

Phase 4 — Circular Bioeconomy

Integrating algae technologies into broader circular systems supporting sustainable industry and climate resilience.
