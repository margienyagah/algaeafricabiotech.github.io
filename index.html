THE OPPORTUNITY

Africa Has the Biomass. AAB Captures the Industrial Value.

East Africa is a global giant in raw seaweed cultivation, yet 95% of our marine biomass is exported raw for pennies , while local agricultural and manufacturing sectors import processed seaweed extracts back at a 20x price markup.

Seaweed is not a low-value commodity. It is a sophisticated natural bio-factory.

A single harvest contains a rich matrix of organic mineral salts, growth-promoting phytohormones, functional gelling polymers and structural cellulose.

Our mission is to end this value drain.

AAB's proprietary wet-processing technology is built to:

  • Maximize Local Value
    Keep high-margin extraction and processing value inside Africa.
  • Eliminate Post-Harvest Waste
    Process fresh biomass immediately, reducing weather-driven drying losses.
  • Deliver Local Supply Security
    Supply regional manufacturers with locally produced bio-inputs and stabilizers.
THE VALUE OPPORTUNITY

The opportunity is not simply to produce more seaweed. It is to transform the same biomass into multiple higher-value products before its value leaves the region.

OUR APPROACH

One Biomass. Multiple Value Streams.

Our cascaded model is designed to extract multiple products from the same fresh seaweed harvest.

01
🌊

Climate-Resilient Biomass

Fresh Eucheuma denticulatum

Sourcing abundant, highly resilient Eucheuma denticulatum (Siphoni) directly from Kwale and East African seaweed farming cooperatives.

Buying fresh and wet is designed to de-risk supply from drying losses while strengthening direct relationships with coastal producers.

02
⚙️

Beachfront Mechanical Pressing

Mobile screw-press technology

Deploying solar-powered mobile screw-presses in modified shipping containers at the beachfront.

Fresh seaweed is mechanically pressed immediately after harvest to extract liquid sap without relying on energy-intensive thermal drying.

03
🌱

High-Performance Agricultural Sap

Liquid biostimulant pathway

Formulating the concentrated liquid fraction with organic-allowed stabilizers.

The resulting biostimulant is being developed around naturally occurring potassium and plant-growth-associated compounds including auxins and cytokinins.

04
🧪

Clean Hydrocolloid Recovery

Iota-Carrageenan

Processing the pre-purified solid press-cake through solar-thermal heated alkaline digestion to recover premium iota-carrageenan.

The target applications include regional food, dairy and oral-care manufacturers requiring functional hydrocolloid stabilizers.

05
♻️

Circular Materials & Bioplastics

Cellulose residue valorization

Upcycling remaining insoluble seaweed cellulose microfibrils after higher-value fractions have been recovered.

Research includes blending seaweed cellulose with local cassava starch and vegetable glycerol to develop compostable agricultural packaging and seedling pots.

06
🔬

Platform Scale-Up

Modular algae processing network

Establishing a scalable logistics and processing footprint that can expand beyond the initial seaweed feedstock.

The longer-term platform is designed to onboard additional marine and microalgal feedstocks where technical and commercial evidence supports expansion.

WHY CASCADE?

The Economics of Biomass Maximization

The commercial logic is to capture more value from the same feedstock while reducing unnecessary energy, waste and supply-chain costs.

01

Multiplied Revenue per Ton

Instead of selling raw seaweed for approximately $0.30/kg , cascading wet biomass creates potential access to higher-value biostimulant and iota-carrageenan markets.

Target product economics include approximately $8.00/L for liquid biostimulants and $10.00/kg for iota-carrageenan.

The objective is to multiply revenue generated from each ton of biomass.

02

Drastically Lower CAPEX & Energy

By pressing seaweed fresh and wet, the model is designed to eliminate the energy-intensive thermal drying step required by conventional seaweed processing.

Beachfront Spokes are designed around modular solar PV and battery storage for off-grid processing.

03

Diversified Market Resilience

AAB is developing products for multiple markets, including agricultural inputs and industrial stabilizers.

Diversification creates the potential to reduce dependence on the economics of a single product category.

04

Zero Liquid Discharge

Our process concept incorporates recirculation and concentration of process wash-water.

Residual organic compounds can be evaluated for recovery and reuse in agricultural product streams rather than treated purely as waste.

ABOUT US

Building Africa's Capacity to Create More Value from Algae

Algae Africa Biotech (AAB) is an Africa-focused biotechnology venture developing a cascaded seaweed biorefinery platform.

Our initial focus is East African seaweed, beginning with Eucheuma denticulatum (Spinosum).

We combine algae biotechnology, wet-processing, product development, commercial intelligence and circular bioeconomy thinking to transform locally available biomass into competitive higher-value products.
