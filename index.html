EAST AFRICAN SEAWEED • CASCADED BIOREFINERY

Unlocking More Value from Africa's Algae.

From coastal biomass to multiple higher-value products.

Algae Africa Biotech is developing a modular seaweed biorefinery platform designed to unlock more value from East Africa's marine biomass.

We are beginning with Eucheuma denticulatum (Spinosum) and investigating a cascaded processing model that can separate the biomass into multiple potential value streams.

OUR GOAL

Extract more value from every kilogram of seaweed while reducing biomass waste.

Explore Our Approach Partner With Us
THE OPPORTUNITY

Africa Has the Biomass. The Opportunity Is in the Value Addition.

East Africa has an established seaweed sector, yet much of the potential value of marine biomass lies beyond the sale of raw or minimally processed feedstock.

Seaweed can be more than a commodity biomass.

The same feedstock can contain multiple potentially useful fractions, including minerals, bioactive compounds, hydrocolloids and structural biomass.

The challenge is to develop processing systems that can:

  • Increase the value captured locally
  • Make better use of available biomass
  • Reduce processing waste
  • Develop products for identifiable markets
  • Build commercially viable processing economics
AAB'S APPROACH

We are exploring whether a cascaded biorefinery can convert one seaweed feedstock into several commercially relevant product streams.

THE BIOREFINERY CONCEPT

A Cascaded Processing Pathway

Our proposed model is designed to investigate how successive processing steps can unlock additional value from the same biomass.

FEEDSTOCK East African Seaweed Eucheuma denticulatum
↓
PROCESS 01 Mechanical Fractionation Crushing / pressing
↓
LIQUID FRACTION Agricultural Extract Bioactive / biostimulant research
↓
Higher-value agricultural applications
SOLID FRACTION Hydrocolloid Recovery Carrageenan / hydrocolloid research
↓
REMAINING FRACTION Residual Biomass Materials characterization
↓
Bio-based materials research
WHY CASCADE?

More Value from the Same Biomass

Rather than optimizing seaweed around only one end product, the cascaded model investigates whether sequential recovery can improve overall biomass economics.

01

Higher Value Capture

Multiple viable products could potentially create greater value than selling the original biomass alone.

02

Better Resource Utilization

Fractions left after one process may become inputs for another rather than becoming immediate waste.

03

Multiple Revenue Opportunities

A diversified product portfolio may reduce dependence on the economics of a single product market.

04

Circular Processing

Process water, biomass residues and other streams can be evaluated for recovery, reuse and further valorization.

05

Modular Scaling

Processing capacity can be developed progressively as biomass supply, technology and customer demand are validated.

OUR COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESIS
The opportunity may not be in producing one seaweed product. It may be in extracting several valuable products from the same biomass through a coordinated processing system.

We are building the technical and commercial evidence required to determine whether that hypothesis can become a viable business.

CURRENT STAGE

From Hypothesis to Commercial Proof

AAB is currently at the development and validation stage. Before major infrastructure investment, we are focused on answering five critical questions.

01

Biomass

Is sufficient Eucheuma denticulatum available at the required quality, consistency and delivered cost?

02

Process

Can the biomass be fractionated efficiently while maintaining commercially useful product characteristics?

03

Products

Can resulting fractions meet the technical and quality specifications required by target markets?

04

Economics

Can biomass costs, processing costs, yields and potential selling prices support viable unit economics?

05

Market

Are there identifiable customers with commercially relevant demand for the resulting products?

OUR FIRST MILESTONE

Not a factory.

Our first milestone is to validate the biomass → process → product → customer → economics pathway and establish evidence that justifies pilot-scale development.

ABOUT AAB

Building Africa's Capacity to Create More Value from Algae

Algae Africa Biotech (AAB) is an Africa-focused biotechnology venture developing approaches for the higher-value utilization of algae biomass.

Our initial focus is East African seaweed, beginning with Eucheuma denticulatum (Spinosum).

We combine algae biotechnology, process development, commercial intelligence and circular bioeconomy thinking to investigate how locally available biomass can become competitive higher-value products.
