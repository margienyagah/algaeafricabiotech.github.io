AFRICAN ALGAE BIOTECHNOLOGY • CASCADING BIOREFINERY

Unlocking the Potential of Africa's Algae Through Biotechnology. Developing technologies and value chains that transform African seaweed and, over time, microalgae into higher-value agricultural inputs and bio-based products.

From fresh seaweed biomass to multiple potential value streams.

Algae Africa Biotech is an African algae biotechnology and biorefinery development company. Our initial focus is the wet-processing and mechanical fractionation of fresh seaweed, beginning with Kappaphycus alvarezii (Cottonii) and Eucheuma denticulatum (Spinosum).

We are developing a cascading processing approach that first investigates the agricultural potential of the liquid fraction, while preparing the scientific and technical foundation for the subsequent valorisation of press cake and other residual biomass.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Africa Has the Biomass. AAB Is Developing the Technology to Unlock Its Value.

Africa's seaweed resources offer opportunities for developing agricultural inputs, functional ingredients and bio-based materials. Yet the value of these resources depends not only on biomass production, but also on the ability to process, characterise and develop products that meet real market requirements.

Much of the opportunity lies in building the technical and commercial capabilities to move beyond the supply of raw biomass towards locally developed products and processing technologies.

AAB is addressing this opportunity through an initial focus on two commercially important red seaweed species: Kappaphycus alvarezii (Cottonii) and Eucheuma denticulatum (Spinosum).

Our approach begins with understanding the feedstock, developing a practical wet-processing pathway and investigating the potential applications of the fractions recovered.

The opportunity AAB is working to address:

  • Local Value Addition
    Develop processing and product-development capabilities closer to seaweed-producing communities.
  • Fresh Biomass Processing
    Investigate wet-processing approaches that reduce reliance on conventional drying before initial processing.
  • Product Development
    Evaluate recovered fractions for agricultural and other potential applications.
  • Evidence-Based Commercialisation
    Connect technical performance, customer requirements and production economics before commercial expansion.
THE VALUE OPPORTUNITY

The opportunity is not simply to produce more seaweed. It is to develop the technologies and knowledge needed to transform locally available biomass into useful, higher-value products.

OUR APPROACH

A Cascading Biorefinery Approach. Designed Around the Whole Biomass.

AAB's approach is to understand and process the biomass in stages, prioritising the first technically and commercially relevant pathway while investigating opportunities for subsequent fraction utilisation.

01
🌊

Feedstock Assessment

Two initial seaweed species

Assessing the availability, characteristics and suitability of fresh Kappaphycus alvarezii (Cottonii) and Eucheuma denticulatum (Spinosum).

Feedstock assessment includes biomass quality, seasonal availability, harvest practices and post-harvest handling.

02
⚙️

Wet-Processing Development

Mechanical fractionation

Developing and evaluating mechanical processing methods to separate useful liquid and solid fractions from fresh seaweed biomass.

03
🌱

Initial Product Development

Agricultural biostimulants

Prioritising the liquid fraction as the first product-development pathway, with a focus on potential agricultural biostimulant applications.

Product characterisation, formulation, stability and crop-response testing will inform further development.

04
♻️

Residual Biomass Research

Subsequent valorisation

Investigating press cake and other residual fractions after the initial processing pathway has been characterised and validated.

Further research will assess technically suitable recovery, utilisation and material-development opportunities.

DEVELOPMENT PATHWAYS

A Clear Sequence for Product and Platform Development

AAB's development priorities are organised according to technical maturity, validation requirements and long-term strategic opportunity.

🌱 INITIAL PRIORITY

Seaweed-Derived Agricultural Biostimulants

First product-development pathway

Investigating the liquid fraction recovered from fresh seaweed wet-processing for potential use in agricultural biostimulant formulations.

Development Focus

  • Liquid fraction characterisation
  • Physicochemical properties
  • Formulation and product stability
  • Crop-response and efficacy trials
  • Customer and application requirements

Validation Objective

Establish whether the recovered liquid fraction can be developed into a consistent, effective and commercially relevant agricultural input.

♻️ SUBSEQUENT RESEARCH

Press-Cake and Residual Biomass Valorisation

Further research after initial pathway validation

Assessing the composition and processing characteristics of the solid fraction and other residual biomass generated through the initial seaweed processing pathway.

Research Focus

  • Residual biomass characterisation
  • Recovery and separation opportunities
  • Potential hydrocolloid applications
  • Structural and material components
  • Technical and economic feasibility

Research Objective

Identify viable ways to use residual biomass without assuming specific products, recovery yields or commercial performance before testing.

🔬 LONG-TERM EXPANSION

  • Potential product applications
  • Technical and commercial feasibility

Expansion Objective

Build on the knowledge and capabilities developed through the initial seaweed platform before pursuing microalgae biorefinery development.

WHY CASCADING MATTERS

Developing More Value from the Same Biomass

A cascading biorefinery approach considers the whole feedstock and evaluates how different fractions may be used through a sequence of technically appropriate processes.

01

Whole-Biomass Thinking

Rather than focusing on a single product, AAB considers how the liquid and solid fractions can be characterised and evaluated for different applications.

02

Practical Process Development

Beginning with fresh-seaweed wet-processing creates an opportunity to investigate mechanical fractionation and processing requirements before committing to larger infrastructure.

03

Multiple Development Opportunities

Agricultural biostimulants are the initial priority, while residual biomass research may create additional opportunities as technical evidence develops.

04

Evidence Before Expansion

Process performance, product quality, customer acceptance and production economics must be assessed before further investment and commercial scale-up.

THE CASCADING BIOREFINERY PRINCIPLE
Start with the most relevant fraction. Understand the whole biomass. Develop additional value pathways as evidence allows.

For AAB, cascading is a development philosophy: prioritising practical validation while keeping the wider potential of the biomass in view.

ABOUT ALGAE AFRICA BIOTECH

Building Africa's Capacity to Create More Value from Algae

Algae Africa Biotech (AAB) is an African algae biotechnology and biorefinery development company focused on developing technologies and value chains for the utilisation of locally available algal resources.

Our initial technical focus is the wet-processing and mechanical fractionation of fresh seaweed, beginning with Kappaphycus alvarezii (Cottonii) and Eucheuma denticulatum (Spinosum).

Our first product-development priority is the investigation of seaweed-derived agricultural biostimulants from the liquid fraction. We also recognise the potential importance of the solid fraction and plan to investigate its further valorisation as the initial processing pathway develops.

AAB's longer-term ambition is to build a broader algae biotechnology platform that can incorporate additional feedstocks, including microalgae, when technical and commercial evidence supports expansion.

OUR MISSION

To develop practical biotechnology and processing solutions that unlock the potential of Africa's algae resources and support higher-value local production.

OUR VISION

A thriving African algae industry built on scientific knowledge, appropriate technology, responsible resource utilisation and competitive bio-based products.
