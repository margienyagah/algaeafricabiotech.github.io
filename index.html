About Us

AlgaeAfrica Biotech is an early-stage biotechnology company developing algae-based solutions for sustainable agriculture and building the future of algae biorefinery in Africa.

The company is currently focused on developing algae-derived biostimulants that improve crop performance and soil health, while laying the foundation for integrated algae biorefineries producing high-value natural pigments, food ingredients, nutraceuticals, and other bio-based products.

Our Mission

To develop and commercialize algae-based technologies that enable sustainable agriculture and accelerate Africa's transition to a circular bioeconomy.

Our Vision

To become Africa’s leading algae biotechnology company, transforming algae biomass into sustainable agricultural and biorefinery products.

Why Choose Us?

We combine biotechnology research with practical product development to create algae-based solutions tailored to African agriculture. Our long-term approach integrates product innovation, local biomass production, and biorefinery development to maximize the value of algae across multiple industries.

Why Algae?

Algae offer one of the most promising biological platforms for building sustainable agriculture and future bio-based industries in Africa.

Sustainable Agriculture

Naturally occurring bioactive compounds from algae can improve crop productivity, soil health, and resource-use efficiency while reducing dependence on synthetic inputs.

Climate Resilience

Algae can be cultivated using limited land and water resources, making them a resilient biomass platform for future climate-smart agriculture.

Circular Bioeconomy

Every component of algae biomass can create value—from agricultural products to natural pigments, food ingredients, and other high-value bio-based products.

African Opportunity

Africa possesses abundant sunlight, extensive coastlines, and growing demand for sustainable agricultural technologies, creating exceptional opportunities for algae innovation.
