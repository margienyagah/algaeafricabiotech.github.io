About Us

AlgaeAfrica Biotechis an early-stage biotechnology company developing algae-based technologies for sustainable agriculture and the emerging bioeconomy. We are beginning with algae-derived biostimulants because they address an immediate agricultural need while creating the cultivation, processing, and technical capabilities required for future algae biorefineries. Our long-term vision is to transform algae into a platform for producing sustainable agricultural inputs, natural ingredients, and other high-value bio-based products in Africa.

The company is currently focused on developing algae-derived biostimulants that improve crop performance and soil health, while laying the foundation for integrated algae biorefineries producing high-value natural pigments, food ingredients, nutraceuticals, and other bio-based products.

Why Algae?

Algae are among the world's most versatile biological resources. They can improve agricultural productivity, produce valuable natural compounds, recycle nutrients, capture carbon, and serve as renewable biomass for a wide range of industries. For Africa, algae represent an opportunity to build sustainable technologies using locally available biological resources rather than imported raw materials.

Sustainable Agriculture

Naturally occurring bioactive compounds from algae can improve crop productivity, soil health, and resource-use efficiency while reducing dependence on synthetic inputs.

Climate Resilience

Algae can be cultivated using limited land and water resources, making them a resilient biomass platform for future climate-smart agriculture.

Circular Bioeconomy

Every component of algae biomass can create value—from agricultural products to natural pigments, food ingredients, and other high-value bio-based products.

African Opportunity

Africa possesses abundant sunlight, extensive coastlines, and growing demand for sustainable agricultural technologies, creating exceptional opportunities for algae innovation.
